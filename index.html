Why You Should Attend

DICE — the Developers & Investors Conference & Exhibition — brings together the people who build and the people who fund across real estate and infrastructure. You get a focused conference programme and an exhibition floor in one place: learn from the stage, then walk the floor to discover projects, partners, and solutions.

Sessions cover market direction, financing and structures, delivery and risk, and what is working now for serious operators. The exhibition gives developers pipeline visibility and helps investors and service providers get in front of the right audience.

Networking is intentional — developers, investors, advisors, and industry leaders in one room, so introductions can turn into follow-up meetings, not just handshakes.

What’s In It For You?

  • 01

    Conference plus exhibition: digest high-signal content in sessions, then explore the floor for projects, products, and introductions that match your mandate

  • 02

    Capital and development in the same conversation—structures, returns, timelines, and how serious investors and serious developers align

  • 03

    Practical execution themes: planning, delivery, costs, regulation, and scaling teams in today’s environment

  • 04

    Trusted networking across developers, investors, brokers, corporates, and advisors—people who show up to do business

  • 05

    A clearer picture of demand, corridors, and asset types so you can position your next site, fund, or partnership with confidence

This Event Is Perfect For:

  • Real estate developers presenting or sourcing projects, joint ventures, and growth capital
  • Active and prospective investors, family offices, and funds allocating to property and real assets
  • Residential, commercial, and mixed-use operators who want capital and market intelligence
  • Entrepreneurs scaling in property and project owners who benefit from exhibition visibility
  • Agents, brokers, advisers, and corporate teams who serve developers and investors
