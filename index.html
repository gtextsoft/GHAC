Why You Should Attend

The Real Estate Investors Developers Conference is built for the two groups who move the market: those who invest and those who develop. In one day we connect capital with credible projects, ideas with execution, and people who can work together long after the event ends.

Sessions span market outlooks, financing and structures, development strategy, asset performance, and emerging themes—always with an eye on what you can apply on Monday morning.

Between panels, expect curated networking so you meet investors, developers, advisors, and decision-makers aligned with your goals—not a generic crowd, but a room full of practitioners.
