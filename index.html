Why You Should Attend

The Developer and Investors Conference and Exhibition is built around two audiences that keep the market moving: those who develop and those who invest. You get both a focused conference programme and an exhibition environment so you can learn from the stage and walk the floor to see projects, partners, and solutions in one place.

Sessions cover market direction, financing and structures, delivery and risk, and what is working now for serious operators. The exhibition adds visibility for developers showcasing pipeline and for investors and service providers who want to be discovered.

Networking is intentional: you are in the room with developers, investors, advisors, and principals who expect substance—so introductions can turn into follow-up meetings, not just handshakes.
